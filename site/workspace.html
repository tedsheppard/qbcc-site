Questions

What is a credit?

A credit is a unit of AI use. 100 credits cover about $30 of AI cost, so a search answer costs a fraction of a credit and a fully researched draft costs more. Each user gets 100 credits a month with their subscription, and you can see the balance and what used it in Settings.

What happens when my credits run out?

Searching by Boolean query, reading and exporting your documents never use credits. Once a user has used their 100 credits, AI work is charged pay as you go, and you are warned at 80% and 100% before that happens.

What counts as a submission for storage?

One uploaded adjudication application, response or further submission, including its annexures if they are in the same file. Your firm's first 500 are included. After that each stored submission is $1.20 a month, for the firm, not per user.

Is my firm's library private?

Your library belongs to your firm and is visible only to the people you invite. Matters in Projects are private to their creator unless shared. Documents are not used to train models. Sopal Workspace is a separate account system from Sopal Decisions on sopal.com.au.

Which AI models does it use?

Sopal Workspace runs on leading models from OpenAI and Anthropic, with Sopal's own retrieval and drafting layers on top. Your firm's precedents, the legislation, case law and adjudication decisions are what the models are given to read.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Your library stays available to export for a period after you cancel.
