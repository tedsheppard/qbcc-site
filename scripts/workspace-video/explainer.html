C.  THE PAYMENT SCHEDULE

29.The claimant gave its payment claim to the respondent by email on 9 July 2024, to the address nominated in the contract.

30.The respondent had 15 business days to respond with a payment schedule under s 76 of the Act.

31.The payment schedule was given late, on the sixteenth business day after the payment claim was given.

32.The respondent therefore became liable to pay the claimed amount on the due date, and may not give an adjudication response.

33.The claimant relies on the statutory declaration of its project manager and the emails exhibited to it.
