Your firm’s adjudication submissions, in one place,
and a way to draft the next one from them.

Most firms keep years of submissions
in folders on a shared drive.

Finding how the firm argued a point last time
usually means opening them one at a time.

Drag in a folder or a zip.

Each submission is read and filed by type, parties, State, amount claimed and date. Scanned pages are read as well.

Search it the way you search case law.

AND, OR, NOT, phrases and proximity, across every submission the firm has filed.

Draft the next submission.

Export to Word in your firm’s format.

Font, spacing, paragraph numbering and page numbers are set once, for everyone.
